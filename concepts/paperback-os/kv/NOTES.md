## Gate reminders

- Brand/title blocks stay **abstract** (no real titles/authors, no readable product names).
- Food worlds must read as **planets first** (atmosphere, terminator) while remaining food-identifiable.
- If psychedelic heat could survive alone as a concert poster, dial it back.
