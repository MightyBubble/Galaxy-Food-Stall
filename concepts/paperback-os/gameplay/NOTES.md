# Paperback OS — Gameplay Concepts

Paranoid Sci-Fi Paperback primary (nicotine cream / brown-black / faded teal; matte airbrush; paper grain; halftone; misregister). Psychedelic wavy ornaments **corners only**. No CRT, no neon glass cockpit, no readable logos. Abstract glyphs OK. Food planets stay food-readable.

## Gate reminders

- No readable English/Chinese labels on crates, cans, or pouches.
- Atmospheric window is a physical sky-ring, not cockpit glass.
- Psychedelic heat stays in corner ornaments (plus one liquid band if needed).
