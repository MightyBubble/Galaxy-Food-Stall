# Visual Language Reference

## 1970s Psychedelic Poster

Use this when the image should feel ecstatic, edible, cosmic, and poster-like.

Core cues:

- Symmetrical or radial composition with one dominant orbit, mandala, sunburst, or portal.
- Liquid contours, wavy orbital bands, melted landscape edges, and organic silhouettes.
- Saturated but controlled palette: burnt orange, acid yellow, avocado green, deep violet, cream, and hot coral. Use 5-7 colors, not a full rainbow.
- Screenprint look: flat ink shapes, overprint seams, slight offset registration, halftone shadows, paper tooth, softened ink edges.
- Typography influence without text: letterform-like curves, swollen borders, nested ribbons, scalloped frames, poster cartouche shapes.
- Food planets can become cosmic bodies: sauce rivers as orbital bands, cheese holes as craters, cabbage veins as continental ridges, vinegar jelly as translucent dark moon, ketchup as red solar sea.

Composition recipes:

- `radial-feast`: central food sun with smaller food planets in orbital rings; dense but readable.
- `cosmic-menu-mandala`: ten planets arranged as a mandala around an empty central void or portal.
- `edible-solar-system`: diagonal sweep of planets, largest foreground body, smaller receding planets.
- `poster-idol`: one oversized food planet as the icon, framed by liquid ribbons and starburst rays.

Hard avoids:

- Generic rainbow gradient, tie-dye blur, modern festival poster, vector smoothness, children-book cuteness, glossy 3D spheres, realistic food photography, UI chrome, technical HUD, literal readable labels.

Prompt shard:

`1970s psychedelic screenprint poster, limited burnt-orange acid-yellow avocado-green cream deep-violet palette, liquid orbital bands, radial poster hierarchy, flat ink shapes, offset registration, halftone paper grain, cosmic food planets, no readable text, no glossy 3D`

## Paranoid Sci-Fi Paperback

Use this when the image should feel like a strange vintage speculative paperback cover: calm, uncanny, literary, slightly bureaucratic, and reality-broken.

Core cues:

- One impossible central image: a food planet hovering above a suburban table, a diner booth under alien stars, a planet sliced by a fake horizon, or a human-scale measuring instrument aimed at a meal-world.
- Strong cover hierarchy: empty upper band or lower band, central surreal object, deep negative space, one stark horizon or frame.
- Limited muted palette: nicotine cream, faded teal, oxidized orange, brown-black, dull red, and dusty yellow.
- Paperback print look: airbrushed gradients flattened into print, torn paper grain, browned edges, misregistered ink, small halftone, matte varnish.
- Reality-instability symbols: duplicate moons, surveillance lens, barcode-like landscape, impossible shadows, floating domestic objects, segmented sky, schematic orbit rings, anonymous architecture.
- Humor should be dry and eerie, not cute: the food is ordinary, the universe treats it as a suspicious artifact.

Composition recipes:

- `domestic-cosmos`: one planet over a diner table or kitchen counter; the room perspective contradicts space.
- `bureaucratic-orbit`: food planet in a diagnostic frame, but not a modern UI; use analog chart marks, ruler ticks, index-card blocks.
- `split-reality-horizon`: top half cosmic void, bottom half flat domestic interior, one planet crossing the seam.
- `surveillance-still-life`: food planet observed by lens, antenna, or anonymous machine, with quiet negative space.

Hard avoids:

- Exact PKD book title, author name, publisher marks, copying an existing cover, modern cyberpunk neon, slick spaceship concept art, action-movie lighting, readable text soup, polished 3D, direct CRT interface windows.

Prompt shard:

`vintage 1970s speculative paperback cover, paranoid literary science fiction mood, impossible food planet still life, muted nicotine-cream teal oxidized-orange brown-black palette, matte airbrush print, paper grain, halftone, misregistered ink, stark negative space, reality fracture, no real title or author`

## Hybrid (Canonical Mix: 70 / 30)

**Canonical ratio:** `paranoid-sci-fi-paperback` **70%** + `seventies-psychedelic-poster` **30%**.

Full production bible (planets, monsters, food, UI, UAT): `../art-bible-hybrid-70-30.md`.

What each side contributes:

| Share | Source | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| 70% | Paranoid Sci-Fi Paperback | Mood, cover hierarchy, negative space, reality fracture, surveillance/measurement symbols, muted paper field, dry uncanny humor |
| 30% | 1970s Psychedelic Poster | Local color heat (burnt orange / acid yellow / avocado), one liquid orbital band or soft overprint seam, edible organic contours — never full mandala takeover |

Hard rule: if the image could pass as a concert poster after removing the food planets, the psychedelic share is too high. If it looks like a CRT widget sheet, restart from the paperback base.

Composition recipes for hybrid:

- `domestic-cosmos-heat`
- `specimen-under-lens`
- `ledger-orbit`
- `stall-at-event-horizon`

Prompt shard:

`lost 1970s experimental science-fiction paperback cover as primary mood, paranoid literary staging, strong negative space, domestic object vs cosmic food planet, surveillance lens or analog measurement ticks, reality fracture, matte airbrush print, nicotine-cream teal oxidized paper grain, halftone, misregistered ink, secondary 1970s psychedelic poster heat only: limited burnt-orange acid-yellow avocado accent, one liquid orbital band, soft overprint seam, no full mandala takeover, cosmic food planets readable as food and as worlds, no modern UI, no CRT windows, no glossy 3D, no real book titles`
